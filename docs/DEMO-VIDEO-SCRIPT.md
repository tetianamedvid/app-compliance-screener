# Demo Video Script — App Compliance Screener

Record a 60–90 second screen recording following the scenes below. Use **QuickTime** (built into your Mac: File > New Screen Recording) or **Loom** (free at loom.com — records and gives you a shareable link).

**Tip:** Loom is the easiest option for sharing via email — it gives you a link and a thumbnail. No file attachment needed.

---

## Before you start

1. Open Chrome.
2. Go to `https://app-compliance-screener-tihxaacrkagcy2ijutdvsk.streamlit.app`.
3. Wait for the page to fully load.
4. Start your screen recording.

---

## Scene 1 — Open the app (5 seconds)

- **Show:** The full browser window with the screener loaded.
- **What the viewer sees:** The title "App Compliance Screener", the URL input box, and the Screen button.

## Scene 2 — Paste a URL and screen it (15 seconds)

- **Do:** Click into the URL text box. Type or paste: `https://imperialcasino.base44.app`
- **Do:** Click the **Screen** button.
- **Wait:** The spinner shows "Screening..." — wait until the result appears (5–15 seconds).
- **What the viewer sees:** A verdict card with a red "Not Supportable" badge, the matched policy category (Gambling), and a confidence score.

## Scene 3 — Show the verdict details (10 seconds)

- **Do:** Slowly scroll down past the verdict card so the viewer can read the app name, URL, policy match, and confidence.
- **Do:** If there is a "Signals & metadata" or "Page content" expander, click it open briefly to show what was scraped.

## Scene 4 — Show the findings table (15 seconds)

- **Do:** Scroll down to the **Findings Table** section.
- **What the viewer sees:** The KPI bar (Total Screened, Not Supportable, Restricted, Likely Supportable, Reviewed).
- **Do:** Point out the table with columns: URL, Name, Verdict, Confidence, P&R Index, Review.
- **Do:** Click the **Verdict** filter dropdown and select "Not Supportable" to show only flagged apps. Then set it back to "All".

## Scene 5 — Change a review status (10 seconds)

- **Do:** In the findings table, find any row and click the **Review** dropdown. Change it from "Pending" to "Reviewed" or "Declined".
- **What the viewer sees:** The change saves automatically — no save button needed.

## Scene 6 — View page content (15 seconds)

- **Do:** Scroll below the table to the **dropdown** that says "Select app to view page content".
- **Do:** Pick an app from the dropdown (e.g. "Imperial Casino").
- **Do:** Click the **View content** button.
- **What the viewer sees:** A popup window opens showing the app name, verdict, and the full scraped page text.
- **Do:** Close the popup by clicking outside it or pressing the X.

## Scene 7 — End (5 seconds)

- **Do:** Scroll back to the top of the page.
- **What the viewer sees:** The clean screener ready for the next URL.
- Stop recording.

## After recording

- **Loom:** Copy the shareable link and paste it into the email. Loom auto-generates a thumbnail preview.
